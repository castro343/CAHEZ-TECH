## 5. BRS forms and incorporation sequence

Use the live eCitizen Companies Registry workflow and generated forms. BRS lists the following relevant forms:
- **CR1** — application to register a company.
- **CR2** — model memorandum for a company with share capital.
- **CR8** — notice of registered office / director residential address information as presented in the current filing workflow.
- **Statement of nominal capital** — generated/required in the registration process.
- **BOF1 / beneficial ownership register filing** — confirm the current portal's exact form and timing.
- **Articles of association** — adopt applicable model articles or have tailored articles reviewed.
- Other declarations/consents generated by the portal.

Workflow:
1. Log in to eCitizen and open BRS Companies Registry.
2. Apply for private limited company registration and submit name preferences.
3. Complete company, registered office, director, member, share and beneficial ownership particulars.
4. Review every generated document against the data entered.
5. Obtain required signatures and upload signed forms.
6. Pay the amount shown by the live portal and retain the receipt.
7. Monitor the application and respond to BRS queries.
8. Download the certificate of incorporation, approved constitution/articles and filed records.
9. Verify the registered particulars and store a controlled digital statutory record book.

BRS's currently published fee schedule lists KES 10,650 for private limited company registration and an estimated 3–5 days. Older BRS FAQ material and other government guidance display different totals. Use the amount and line items shown at the live eCitizen checkout; budget for possible additional services and professional fees.

## 6. Post-incorporation compliance checklist

Immediately after incorporation:
- Obtain and securely store certificate of incorporation and company registration documents.
- Confirm company KRA PIN and tax obligations with KRA/accountant.
- Open a company bank account using the bank's requirements; keep company and personal funds separate.
- Establish statutory registers: members, directors, beneficial owners, share allotments/transfers, charges and minutes/resolutions as applicable.
- Adopt founder/director resolutions for banking, accounting reference date, registered office, delegated authority and initial contracts.
- Set up bookkeeping, invoicing, expense evidence and tax calendar.
- Confirm annual return filing deadlines and beneficial ownership update obligations.
- Confirm whether a company secretary is required as the company grows.
- Review NSSF, SHA, PAYE, employment and workplace obligations before hiring.
- Register with ODPC as controller and/or processor if required for actual processing activities; assess any exemption carefully.
- Prepare privacy notice, data-processing agreements, retention schedule, incident response and security controls before accepting customer personal data.
- Obtain suitable insurance and professional advice as business activity expands.

## 7. Intellectual property and contracts

- Record who created the logo, code, copy, product designs and other assets.
- Use written IP assignment agreements for employees and contractors; payment alone does not automatically settle every ownership question.
- Use confidentiality and invention-assignment terms for contractors and collaborators.
- Register relevant trademarks through KIPI after clearance; consider word mark and logo protection and appropriate Nice classes.
- Protect software through copyright and controlled source-code access; consider patent/design advice only where the invention qualifies.
- Prepare customer terms, SaaS subscription terms, acceptable-use policy, privacy notice, service-level terms and data-processing agreement.
- Avoid publishing confidential inventions before receiving IP advice.
